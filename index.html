About

My work sits at the intersection of AI agents, persistent memory, and system security.

I am interested in how information moves between autonomous agents, how it is stored and retrieved over time, and how those processes can produce delayed or cross-agent failures. I build reproducible evaluations that connect precise threat models with observable system behavior.

Research

Three connected questions guide my current work.

Memory integrity

When can one agent alter what another agent remembers?

Studying persistent memory poisoning, trust boundaries, and the conditions under which untrusted information becomes accepted experience.

Propagation

How do failures travel across agents?

Tracing indirect influence through communication, delegation, and private memory stores.

Evaluation

What evidence makes an agent security claim credible?

Designing measurable benchmarks for persistence, delayed impact, and cross-agent effects.
